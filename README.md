# Cheap FPV Drone: Reverse-Engineered Control + RL/CV Testbed

A sub-$50 Temu FPV camera drone, fully reverse-engineered from scratch into a controllable, observable platform for applying
reinforcement learning and computer vision to real hardware. Everything
here, the control protocol, the video pipeline, the camera switching, was
recovered through packet capture, Android app decompilation, and real
flight testing.

## progress so far .

- **Full flight control over WiFi**, 
- **Two-stage arm/launch gesture**, 
- **Live video feed, fully decoded and corrected.** 
- **Forward/downward camera switching.** 
- **Telemetry channel checked and confirmed absent for environment observation**
